CPU & Memory Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

CPU & Memory Tracker is a macOS app that shows your Mac's CPU and memory usage. Your privacy is important, so the app is designed to collect nothing.

Data we collect

None. CPU & Memory Tracker does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.

How the app works

The app reads system-wide CPU and memory statistics directly from macOS on your Mac. These readings are shown on screen and are never saved or sent anywhere. The app makes no network connections.

Settings

Your preferences (such as warning percentages, Always on Top, and Launch at Login) are stored locally on your Mac using standard macOS settings storage. They never leave your device.

Third parties

The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party services.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
